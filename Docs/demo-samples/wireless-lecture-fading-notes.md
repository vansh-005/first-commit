# Wireless Communications — Lecture 4 notes: fading

- **Fading** is the variation of received signal strength over time, distance or frequency.
- **Multipath fading** happens when reflected copies of a signal arrive with different delays and
  interfere destructively at the receiver.
- **Large-scale fading** (path loss and shadowing) changes slowly with distance and obstacles.
- **Small-scale fading** changes rapidly over fractions of a wavelength.
- Mitigation: diversity (time, frequency, space), equalization, and error-correcting codes.

Exam tip: be ready to explain the difference between flat fading and frequency-selective fading.
